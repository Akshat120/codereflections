Reflection Rule

How to turn one hard solve into durable skill.

After a difficult problem

Don't immediately force another problem of the same difficulty. Slow down and extract the lesson while it is still fresh.

Spend 10–15 minutes writing down:

  • Observation — the key idea that unlocked the problem.
  • Mistake — what made you stuck and how you escaped.
  • Pattern — the algorithm family or technique name.
  • Future recognition — what you will look for next time.
  • Implementation — the simplest clean way to code it.

These map directly to the five questions on the Reflect page.

How the app works
  1. Load Problem: Paste any Codeforces URL or code (e.g., 1904A) on the Load page. Metadata, rating, and tags are fetched automatically.
  2. Solve & Track: Open the problem on Codeforces and tackle it while the live stopwatch runs on the Problem page.
  3. Reflect Deeply: Complete five structured reflection prompts on the Reflect page before moving on.
  4. Review & Master: Search, filter, and review your historical insights in your personal Progress.
